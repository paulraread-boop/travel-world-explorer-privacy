Travel World Explorer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Travel World Explorer is an Android application for discovering TV, sports and other online resources by country. This policy explains the app's features and the privacy considerations associated with linked services.

Information and device access

The Android app requests internet access. Its code includes a WebView with JavaScript and browser storage enabled. The app can list installed compatible VPN applications and open the selected VPN app or Android VPN settings. This does not mean the app itself provides a VPN connection.

The inspected native code does not include an account registration system, contacts access, location permission, or an advertising SDK. This observation is not a guarantee that no information is processed by linked websites, embedded content, or other services.

Websites, broadcasters and external services

Selecting stations and services can open third-party websites or embedded content. These third parties may receive information such as your IP address, device/browser information, and usage activity under their own privacy policies. Availability may depend on country, login or subscriptions. Travel World Explorer does not control these third-party practices.

Local storage

The app enables browser DOM storage. Website or app-interface settings may be stored on your device. Any further claims about what is stored and how long it remains require testing.

VPN

The VPN feature opens installed VPN apps or Android VPN settings. Travel World Explorer does not itself establish, manage or guarantee a VPN connection. Any VPN provider's privacy practices are governed by its own policy.

Children

This app has not been reviewed or designated as a children-directed service. Its content and external links should be evaluated before selecting a target audience in Google Play Console.

Changes

This policy may be updated if app features, integrations, or data handling change.
